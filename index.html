PRIVACY POLICY FOR MI-SURIA LITE

This Privacy Policy explains how Mi-Suria Lite ("Application"), developed by MIMOS Berhad ("MIMOS", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Application.

Mi-Suria Lite is a mobile application designed to display Photovoltaic Monitoring System (PVMS) information through a dashboard.

1. Login Information

Mi-Suria Lite does not provide user registration or sign-up.

Login credentials, including an email address and password, are provided to users by an authorized administrator.

The login credentials are sent to and processed by MIMOS's server to verify the user's access to the Application.

Users are not required to provide other personal information through the Application.

2. Device Information and Permissions

The Application does not collect information from your mobile device.

The Application does not require or access sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Location
  • Camera
  • Contacts
  • Phone information
  • Device storage

3. Location Information

The Application does not collect or track the location of your mobile device.

Any location shown in the Application is related to the PVMS or solar installation and is not obtained from the user's device location services.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The Application does not provide advertising or user tracking services.

Login information is processed by MIMOS's server for authentication and access control.

The Application does not allow users to share personal information with other users or third parties.

5. Data Retention and Security

Login information is used to authenticate users and control access to the Application.

MIMOS takes reasonable measures to protect information used for authentication and access to the Application.

6. Uninstalling the Application

You may stop using the Application at any time by uninstalling it from your mobile device.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or the applicable age in the user's jurisdiction.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MIMOS may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published through this Privacy Policy page.

9. Your Consent

By using Mi-Suria Lite, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
